# Quote documents (Maor's standing rule)

Every quote type in this CRM, existing or new, renders its sections as collapsible cards that open on a tap: only the header, the notice and the price card stay always visible. Build each section with `Fold` from `src/components/QuoteDoc.tsx` (see `AiQuoteDoc.tsx` for a second product using it). The three fixed packages (Simple, More Simple, Max Simple) are retired: new quotes are "קמפיין AI" or "הרכבה אישית".
